# Interview Templates

## Introduction

In a high-pressure 45-minute coding interview, you do not have time to invent algorithms from scratch. You must have standard templates memorized perfectly.

When you recognize a pattern, your hands should automatically type out the skeleton of the solution before you even fully process the specific edge cases of the problem.

---

## The Muscle Memory Advantage

Memorizing these templates gives you three massive advantages:
1. **Speed**: You can type out the core structure in 30 seconds.
2. **Bug Prevention**: These templates have been designed to avoid classic off-by-one errors and infinite loops (especially in Binary Search and Linked Lists).
3. **Cognitive Offloading**: Once the skeleton is on the screen, your brain is free to focus 100% of its energy on the specific business logic required for the question, rather than worrying about pointer math.

---

## The Templates Included

We have distilled the most robust, Pythonic templates for:
- **Sliding Window** (Variable size with `left`/`right` pointers)
- **Two Pointers** (Converging and Fast/Slow)
- **Binary Search** (The exact `while left <= right:` formulation)
- **DFS / Backtracking** (Recursive skeleton)
- **BFS** (Level-order queue processing)
- **Graphs** (Adjacency list construction and traversal)
- **Heaps** (Top-K tracking)
- **Dynamic Programming** (Top-Down with `@cache`)

Study these until you can write them on a whiteboard without thinking.
